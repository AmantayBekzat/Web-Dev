Omlette

  • Time: 10min
  • Serves: 1
Ingredients Quantity
Eggs 2
Milk 1-2 tbsp
Salt To taste
Pepper To taste
Butter 1 tsp

Step 1: Whisk eggs with milk (or water), salt, and pepper in a bowl.

Step 2: Melt butter in a non-stick pan over medium heat.

Step 3: Pour egg mixture into the pan.

Step 4: Cook until set, add fillings if desired, fold, and serve.

Macaroni and Cheese

  • Time: 30min
  • Serves: 6
Ingredients Quantity
Dried macaroni 4 cups
Cheese (cheddar or mix) 1 lb
Egg 1
Salt&Pepper To taste
Butter 1/4 cup
Milk 2,5 cups
All‑purpose flour 1/4 cup
Mustard 2 tsp

Step 1: Cook the macaroni until very firm and drain.

Step 2: Beat the egg in a small bowl.

Step 3: Melt the butter and whisk in flour over medium heat.

Step 4: Add milk and mustard, cooking until thick.

Step 5: Temper the egg with sauce, then stir in cheese, seasonings, and macaroni; bake until bubbly.

Chicken soup

  • Time: 1hr 45min
  • Serves: 10
Ingredients Quantity
Chicken 3 lb
Carrots (halved) 4
Celery stalks(halved) 4
Onion 1
Salt&Pepper To taste
Chicken bouillon granules 1 tsp

Step 1: Place the chicken, carrots, celery, and onion in a large pot and add enough water to cover.

Step 2: Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer until the chicken meat falls off the bone (about 90 minutes).

Step 3: Remove the chicken, chop the meat, discard bones and skin, and return meat to the pot.

Step 4: Season with salt, pepper, and bouillon if desired, heat through, and serve.
